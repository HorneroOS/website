# Capture slots: how the site shows what HorneroOS looks like

Every product image on the site is a real host capture with provenance.
There are no renders, mock-ups, concept images or captures of the previous
(pre-Signal) appearance. Until a capture exists, its place on the page is a
labelled pending plate that says what will be shown and how it will be
taken.

## The slot is the contract

`src/data/capture-slots.json` is the single source of truth. Each slot
(`CAP-<AREA>-<NN>`, for example `CAP-HERO-01`) declares:

- what must be on screen (`title`, `description`, `alt`, in Spanish and
  English), its capture `session` and `priority` (P0 launch-critical, P1
  important, P2 complementary);
- the exact geometry: `aspect`, `width`, `height`, whether a `scale2x`
  master is wanted, and the `format` (PNG master for screenshots, WebM+MP4
  plus a poster for clips, AVIF/JPG for photographs);
- the staging: `theme`, `mode`, `layout`, `wallpaper`, the `setup` steps to
  reach that state on the capture machine, and the `privacy` rules;
- where it renders: `pages` (root-relative Spanish routes). Every slot also
  renders on the `/showroom` capture wall.
- its `status`: `pending` (with `file: null`) or `captured` (with `file`,
  `sha256`, `capturedAt`, and optional `source`, `pins` and `credit`).

`src/data/captures.ts` types and re-exports the data. Do not hand-edit
`docs/CAPTURE-SHOT-LIST.md`: it is generated from the data file by
`node scripts/gen-shot-list.mjs`, and `npm run check:captures` fails when it
is stale. The shot list groups the slots into capture sessions with the
global environment, privacy and export rules.

## Rendering

One component renders every slot everywhere: `CaptureFigure`
(`src/components/CaptureFigure.astro`).

- A `pending` slot renders an instrument-panel plate in a box with the
  slot's aspect ratio: slot id, "Pending — host capture", priority, what
  must be captured, and the aspect, resolution, theme, mode and format.
  Filling the slot never moves the page.
- A `captured` slot renders the real file (or, for clips, the looping video
  with its poster) with its provenance. `CaptureLabel` states honestly what
  the image is: pending, a real Hornero Signal capture or clip, or a real
  photograph.
- Pages place their hero slots by hand and use `CaptureGallery` for the
  rest of the slots declared in their `pages`. `LookCompare` wipes between
  two slots (Signal Night and Signal Day).

Social cards (`public/og/`, generated by `scripts/build-og.mjs`) never embed
a desktop screenshot; they pair the brand with site data (the showroom card
counts captured slots by priority, from the data file).

Rendered documentation (from HorneroOS/docs, pinned) may still reference the
removed `/images/apps/...` captures. `src/lib/remark-docs-links.mjs`
replaces those images with a pending-capture note naming their slot
(`CAP-TERM-06`, `CAP-TERM-01`) instead of a broken link; point the docs at
the captured asset once the slot is filled.

## Taking and delivering a capture

1. Read the slot's brief in `docs/CAPTURE-SHOT-LIST.md` (global rules
   included): demo user, fixed 1920×1080 scale-1 output, shipped wallpapers
   only, released composition, no personal data anywhere in frame.
2. Shoot it on the capture machine with `scripts/capture-helper.sh`
   (`shot`, `window`, `region`, `clip`, then `verify CAP-ID file`).
3. Fill the slot: `node scripts/capture-ingest.mjs CAP-ID file` validates
   the size and aspect ratio, copies the file to `src/assets/captures/`
   (clips: `public/captures/` plus a poster in `src/assets/captures/`),
   records its checksum and flips `status` to `captured`. Use `--pins`,
   `--source` and `--credit` for provenance; `--reset` returns a slot to
   its pending plate.
4. Run `npm run build` and look at the page: the plate becomes the real
   image in the same box.

Adjust the slot's `alt` text to what the final frame really shows before
ingesting.

## What may stay: kept images

Not every image in the repository is a product capture. Brand marks,
favicons, social cards, third-party application logos shown nominatively,
licensed wallpapers and licensed greeter scene media are listed, with a
category and a reason, in `src/data/kept-images.json`. Anything else image-
or video-like in `public/`, `src/assets/` or `assets/` fails the build.

`npm run check:captures` (run by `npm run build`, which also runs it with
`--dist` over the built pages) enforces all of the above: well-formed
slots, ES/EN copy, captured files present with matching checksums, every
slot id mentioned in source declared, every declared page rendering its
slots in both locales, the shot list up to date, and no unaccounted
showcase image anywhere. The old showroom collection
(`src/content/showroom/`), `public/images/`, `assets/screenshots/` and
`src/assets/concepts/` must not come back.

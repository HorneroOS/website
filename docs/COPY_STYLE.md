# Writing style

How the site's own copy is written. Pinned product data (`src/data/product/`)
keeps its upstream wording; fix that in the source repository, not here.

## Rules

- **Say what it is.** A heading names the thing ("Layouts", "Extensions", "Capture
  ledger"). It is not a slogan, and it does not need a second sentence.
- **No contrast framing.** Avoid "X, not Y" and "less X, more Y" as a rhythm. If a
  limit matters (no ISO, not sandboxed, not an installer image), state it once, in
  the place where a reader needs it.
- **No defensive honesty.** Do not announce that something is real, honest or
  unstaged. Provenance lives in the capture's source line and the showroom ledger.
- **Facts carry a source.** Dates, versions and history link to a commit, tag or
  catalogue entry. The 2018 origin is the maintainer's account and says so.
- **Plain verbs, sentence case.** Prefer "Install on Arch" to a promise. Skip
  "seamless", "powerful", "experience", "journey" and "crafted".
- **No labels that repeat the heading.** Eyebrows, numbered markers and all-caps
  tags appear only when they carry information (a real sequence, a status).
- **No arrows on links.** `↗` is kept for links that leave the site.
- **People are named by role or name, never by guessed pronouns.** Use "they" or
  rewrite the sentence.
- **Names.** The product is "HorneroOS", the art direction is "Hornero 199X".
  Page titles read `Page | HorneroOS`.

## Spanish

The Spanish plate lines ("Archivo de capturas reales", "Transmisión 001") belong to
the 199X identity. They label a page; they do not carry content the English text
lacks.

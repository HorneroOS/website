# Wallpaper variety evidence

The before capture is the theme recipe grid at website main
`1701e5ea10ff893172f0bbdd6f4ce2e152dbf46d`. It listed palette names and
descriptions without showing their desktop rendering. The after capture is
the new gallery of three real wallpaper/color combinations.

Both were rendered in Chromium at 1440 CSS pixels. The change was also
inspected at 390, 768, and 1440 pixels. Anchored navigation now accounts for
the sticky header at phone, tablet, and desktop widths.

The product images are live-host captures from an Arch + Hyprland development
session on an empty workspace. They show a custom dark appearance with
wallpaper-derived expressive colors; they do not claim to be three shipped
theme packs or a Hornero ISO. Per-image source paths, environment pins, image
hashes, and wallpaper names are stored beside the originals in
`assets/screenshots/curated/*.json`.
